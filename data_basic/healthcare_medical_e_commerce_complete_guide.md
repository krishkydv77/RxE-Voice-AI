## PART 1: Healthcare & Medical E-Commerce (Basic to Advanced)

### 1. Introduction to Medical E-Commerce
Medical E-commerce refers to the digital buying and selling of medical products, pharmaceutical drugs, diagnostic services, medical devices, and health consultation services online. Unlike traditional retail e-commerce, healthcare e-commerce involves strict regulatory compliance, patient privacy standards (like HIPAA, GDPR), cold-chain logistics, and digital prescription verifications.

### 2. How Healthcare E-Commerce Works (End-to-End Workflow)
1. **User Onboarding & Authentication:** Secure signup with identity verification.
2. **Product / Service Discovery:** Search engines tuned for medical terminology, dosage filters, and active ingredients.
3. **Prescription Upload & AI Verification:** Users upload prescriptions; OCR (Optical Character Recognition) and registered pharmacists verify legitimacy.
4. **Order Processing & Inventory Check:** Real-time stock check with local hyper-local fulfillment centers or central warehouses.
5. **Payment Gateway Integration:** Insurance coverage application, HSA/FSA card processing, standard digital payments.
6. **Cold-Chain Logistics & Delivery:** Temperature-controlled delivery for insulin, vaccines, and biologics.
7. **Post-Purchase Care:** Automated refill reminders, tele-consultation follow-ups, and adherence tracking.

### 3. Key Business Models in Healthcare E-Commerce
* **B2C Online Pharmacy (e-Pharmacy):** Selling OTC medicines, prescription drugs, and wellness products directly to consumers (e.g., Netmeds, 1mg, PillPack).
* **B2B Medical Supplies:** Selling medical equipment, surgical tools, and consumables directly to hospitals, clinics, and doctors (e.g., ProcureHealth, Amazon Business Healthcare).
* **D2C Healthcare Brands:** Brands manufacturing and selling direct-to-consumer supplements, diagnostics kits, and wellness gear.
* **Aggregator & Telehealth Integration Platforms:** Combining doctor consultation, lab tests, and medicine delivery into one ecosystem (e.g., Practo, Teladoc).

### 4. Technical Architecture & Compliance Checklist
* **HIPAA / GDPR Compliance:** Encrypted storage of Protected Health Information (PHI).
* **Security:** End-to-end SSL/TLS encryption, SOC2 Certification, ISO 27001.
* **Integrations:** Electronic Health Records (EHR/EMR) integration via FHIR/HL7 standards.
* **Logistics:** Temperature-controlled supply chain tracking (IoT sensors).

## PART 2: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: What makes Medical E-commerce different from regular E-commerce?
**A:** Medical E-commerce handles sensitive health data, requires strict prescription validation, deals with regulated substances, and requires specialized temperature-controlled storage and fast delivery timelines.

#### Q2: Can prescription medicines be sold online legally?
**A:** Yes, provided the platform complies with country-specific drug regulations (e.g., FDA in the US, CDSCO in India), requires a valid prescription from a licensed doctor, and uses licensed pharmacists to dispense medicines.

#### Q3: How do platforms manage temperature-sensitive medicines like Insulin?
**A:** Through cold-chain packaging (insulated boxes, gel packs) and IoT-enabled logistics tracking real-time temperature throughout transit.

#### Q4: What is the role of AI in Healthcare E-Commerce?
**A:** AI helps in reading handwritten prescriptions (OCR), predicting medicine stock requirements, personalized health supplement recommendations, and automated customer triage.

### Category 1: Respiratory & Pulmonary Disorders (1–50)

#### 1. Acute Bronchitis
* **Symptoms:** Persistent cough, mucus production, fatigue, mild fever.
* **Suggestions:** Stay hydrated, rest adequately, use humidifiers.
* **Basic Solution:** OTC cough suppressants, mucolytics; antibiotics only if bacterial.

#### 2. Asthma
* **Symptoms:** Wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, night coughing.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid dust, pollen, and smoke; track peak flow rates.
* **Basic Solution:** Inhaled corticosteroids (preventers), short-acting beta-agonists (rescue inhalers).

#### 3. Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
* **Symptoms:** Chronic shortness of breath, frequent respiratory infections, chronic cough.
* **Suggestions:** Quit smoking immediately, pulmonary rehabilitation exercises.
* **Basic Solution:** Bronchodilators, inhaled steroids, supplemental oxygen therapy.

#### 4. Pneumonia
* **Symptoms:** High fever, chills, cough with phlegm, sharp chest pain when breathing.
* **Suggestions:** Rest, high fluid intake, pulse oximetry monitoring.
* **Basic Solution:** Targeted antibiotics (for bacterial), antivirals, fever reducers (Paracetamol).

#### 5. Allergic Rhinitis (Hay Fever)
* **Symptoms:** Sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes, nasal congestion.
* **Suggestions:** Use HEPA air purifiers, avoid known allergens.
* **Basic Solution:** Antihistamines (Cetirizine, Loratadine), nasal steroid sprays.

#### 6. Sinusitis (Sinus Infection)
* **Symptoms:** Facial pressure/pain, nasal blockage, green/yellow discharge, loss of smell.
* **Suggestions:** Steam inhalation, saline nasal rinsing (Neti pot).
* **Basic Solution:** Decongestants, saline sprays, pain relievers, antibiotics if lasting >10 days.

#### 7. Sleep Apnea (Obstructive)
* **Symptoms:** Loud snoring, gasping for air during sleep, morning headache, daytime fatigue.
* **Suggestions:** Weight loss, avoiding alcohol before bed, side-sleeping.
* **Basic Solution:** CPAP (Continuous Positive Airway Pressure) machine, oral appliances.

#### 8. Pulmonary Embolism
* **Symptoms:** Sudden shortness of breath, sharp chest pain, coughing up blood.
* **Suggestions:** Immediate emergency medical attention required.
* **Basic Solution:** Anticoagulants (blood thinners), thrombolytics in severe cases.

#### 9. Pleurisy
* **Symptoms:** Sharp chest pain during breathing or coughing, shortness of breath.
* **Suggestions:** Rest, avoid sudden cold exposure.
* **Basic Solution:** NSAIDs (Ibuprofen) for pain relief, treating underlying cause.

#### 10. Atelectasis
* **Symptoms:** Difficulty breathing, rapid shallow breathing, low oxygen saturation.
* **Suggestions:** Deep breathing exercises, incentive spirometry post-surgery.
* **Basic Solution:** Chest physiotherapy, airway clearance devices.

### Category 2: Cardiovascular & Circulatory Disorders (51–100)

#### 51. Essential Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)
* **Symptoms:** Often asymptomatic; headaches, dizziness, shortness of breath in severe cases.
* **Suggestions:** Low-sodium DASH diet, regular aerobic exercise, stress reduction.
* **Basic Solution:** ACE inhibitors, ARBs, Beta-blockers, Diuretics.

#### 52. Coronary Artery Disease (CAD)
* **Symptoms:** Angina (chest pain), shortness of breath, fatigue during exertion.
* **Suggestions:** Heart-healthy Mediterranean diet, smoking cessation.
* **Basic Solution:** Statins, Antiplatelet drugs (Aspirin), Nitroglycerin, Angioplasty/CABG.

#### 53. Myocardial Infarction (Heart Attack)
* **Symptoms:** Crushing chest pain radiating to arm/jaw, sweating, nausea, shortness of breath.
* **Suggestions:** Emergency medical call immediately (911/112).
* **Basic Solution:** Thrombolytic therapy, emergency PCI (stenting), aspirin.

#### 54. Heart Failure (Systolic / Diastolic)
* **Symptoms:** Swollen legs/ankles (edema), breathlessness while lying flat, chronic fatigue.
* **Suggestions:** Fluid restriction, daily weight monitoring, low salt intake.
* **Basic Solution:** ACE inhibitors/ARNI, Beta-blockers, Aldosterone antagonists, Diuretics.

#### 55. Atrial Fibrillation (AFib)
* **Symptoms:** Heart palpitations, fluttering chest, fatigue, lightheadedness.
* **Suggestions:** Limit caffeine and alcohol intake, monitor heart rate via wearables.
* **Basic Solution:** Antiarrhythmics, blood thinners (DOACs/Warfarin), cardioversion, ablation.

#### 56. Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD)
* **Symptoms:** Leg pain during walking (claudication), cold feet, slow-healing leg sores.
* **Suggestions:** Supervised walking therapy, foot care inspection.
* **Basic Solution:** Antiplatelet medications, Cilostazol, cholesterol-lowering drugs, revascularization.

#### 57. Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)
* **Symptoms:** Unilateral leg swelling, pain, warmth, redness.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid prolonged sitting, wear compression stockings during long flights.
* **Basic Solution:** Anticoagulants (Heparin, Rivaroxaban), thrombolytics.

#### 58. Infective Endocarditis
* **Symptoms:** Fever, chills, heart murmur, fatigue, petechiae on skin.
* **Suggestions:** Good oral hygiene, prophylactic antibiotics before dental procedures if high-risk.
* **Basic Solution:** IV Antibiotics for 4-6 weeks, surgical valve repair/replacement.

#### 59. Aortic Stenosis
* **Symptoms:** Exertional chest pain, fainting (syncope), shortness of breath.
* **Suggestions:** Regular echocardiogram monitoring, avoiding strenuous heavy lifting.
* **Basic Solution:** Surgical Aortic Valve Replacement (SAVR) or TAVR.

#### 60. Raynaud's Phenomenon
* **Symptoms:** Fingers/toes turning white then blue in response to cold or stress.
* **Suggestions:** Keep hands/feet warm, wear gloves, avoid thermal shocks.
* **Basic Solution:** Calcium channel blockers (Nifedipine), topical nitrates.

### Category 3: Gastrointestinal & Digestive Disorders (101–150)

#### 101. Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)
* **Symptoms:** Heartburn, acid regurgitation, chest pain, difficulty swallowing.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid late-night meals, elevate head during sleep, cut spicy/fatty foods.
* **Basic Solution:** Proton Pump Inhibitors (Omeprazole), H2 Blockers, Antacids.

#### 102. Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)
* **Symptoms:** Abdominal cramping, bloating, gas, alternating diarrhea and constipation.
* **Suggestions:** Follow a Low-FODMAP diet, manage stress, increase soluble fiber.
* **Basic Solution:** Antispasmodics (Mebeverine), laxatives or anti-diarrheals, probiotics.

#### 103. Peptic Ulcer Disease
* **Symptoms:** Burning stomach pain, feeling of fullness, intolerance to fatty foods, nausea.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid NSAID overuse, quit smoking, avoid alcohol.
* **Basic Solution:** H. pylori eradication therapy (antibiotics + PPI), cytoprotective agents.

#### 104. Crohn's Disease
* **Symptoms:** Severe diarrhea, abdominal pain/cramping, weight loss, malnutrition.
* **Suggestions:** High-calorie anti-inflammatory diet, avoid trigger foods.
* **Basic Solution:** Corticosteroids, Immunomodulators, Biologics (Infliximab, Adalimumab).

#### 105. Ulcerative Colitis
* **Symptoms:** Bloody diarrhea, rectal bleeding, urgency to defecate, abdominal pain.
* **Suggestions:** Stay hydrated, eat small frequent meals during flare-ups.
* **Basic Solution:** 5-ASA agents (Mesalamine), corticosteroids, biologics, surgery (colectomy).

#### 106. Celiac Disease
* **Symptoms:** Diarrhea, bloating, fatigue, anemia, bone loss due to gluten ingestion.
* **Suggestions:** Strict lifelong 100% gluten-free diet.
* **Basic Solution:** Nutritional supplementation (Iron, Vitamin D, B12), managing gut healing.

#### 107. Gallstones (Cholelithiasis)
* **Symptoms:** Sudden sharp pain in upper right abdomen, nausea, vomiting after fatty meals.
* **Suggestions:** Low-fat diet, gradual weight loss (avoid rapid weight drop).
* **Basic Solution:** Ursodeoxycholic acid (for small stones), Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy.

#### 108. Diverticulitis
* **Symptoms:** Severe left lower abdominal pain, fever, nausea, changes in bowel habits.
* **Suggestions:** Clear liquid diet during acute phase, high-fiber diet in recovery.
* **Basic Solution:** Oral or IV Antibiotics, pain relievers (avoid NSAIDs during acute inflammation).

#### 109. Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD)
* **Symptoms:** Usually asymptomatic; fatigue, mild discomfort in upper right abdomen.
* **Suggestions:** Weight loss (7-10%), aerobic exercise, low-sugar/low-carbohydrate diet.
* **Basic Solution:** Vitamin E, Pioglitazone (in specific patients), managing lipid profile.

#### 110. Gastroparesis
* **Symptoms:** Early fullness, nausea, vomiting of undigested food, abdominal bloating.
* **Suggestions:** Small, low-fiber, low-fat meals, pureed foods, walking after eating.
* **Basic Solution:** Prokinetic agents (Metoclopramide, Domperidone), antiemetics.

*(Conditions 111 to 150 include: Chronic Gastritis, Acute Pancreatitis, Chronic Pancreatitis, Cirrhosis of Liver, Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B, Hepatitis C, Hemorrhoids, Anal Fissure, Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth (SIBO), Achalasia, Esophageal Stricture, Barrett's Esophagus, Gastric Cancer, Colorectal Polyps, Microscopic Colitis, Ischemic Colitis, Short Bowel Syndrome, Ascites, Portal Hypertension, Hepatic Encephalopathy, Primary Biliary Cholangitis, Primary Sclerosing Cholangitis, Gilbert's Syndrome, Hirschsprung's Disease, Intussusception, Volvulus, Meckel's Diverticulum, Malabsorption Syndrome, Tropical Sprue, Whipple's Disease, Cyclic Vomiting Syndrome, Gastric Outlet Obstruction, Eosinophilic Esophagitis, Zenker's Diverticulum, Dysbiosis, Lactose Intolerance, Fructose Malabsorption, Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency, Rectal Prolapse.)*

### Category 4: Endocrine & Metabolic Disorders (151–200)

#### 151. Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus
* **Symptoms:** Excessive thirst (polydipsia), frequent urination (polyuria), weight loss, extreme fatigue.
* **Suggestions:** Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM), carb counting, regular physical activity.
* **Basic Solution:** Lifelong Insulin therapy (basal-bolus regimen or insulin pump).

#### 152. Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
* **Symptoms:** Increased thirst, slow-healing wounds, blurred vision, tingling in hands/feet.
* **Suggestions:** Low glycemic index diet, regular exercise, weight loss.
* **Basic Solution:** Metformin, SGLT2 inhibitors, GLP-1 receptor agonists, Insulin if required.

#### 153. Hypothyroidism (Hashimoto's)
* **Symptoms:** Fatigue, weight gain, cold intolerance, dry skin, constipation, depression.
* **Suggestions:** Adequate iodine/selenium intake, take medication on an empty stomach.
* **Basic Solution:** Levothyroxine (synthetic thyroid hormone replacement).

#### 154. Hyperthyroidism (Graves' Disease)
* **Symptoms:** Weight loss despite increased appetite, rapid heartbeat, tremors, heat intolerance.
* **Suggestions:** High-calorie diet initially, avoid excess iodine intake.
* **Basic Solution:** Antithyroid drugs (Methimazole, PTU), Beta-blockers, Radioactive Iodine, Surgery.

#### 155. Cushing's Syndrome
* **Symptoms:** Moon face, fatty hump between shoulders, purple stretch marks, high blood pressure.
* **Suggestions:** Gradual reduction of corticosteroid medications under supervision.
* **Basic Solution:** Steroid-tapering, surgery to remove cortisol-producing tumors, medication inhibitors.

#### 156. Addison's Disease (Adrenal Insufficiency)
* **Symptoms:** Extreme fatigue, hyperpigmentation of skin, low blood pressure, salt craving.
* **Suggestions:** Carry a medical alert bracelet, increase salt during heavy sweating.
* **Basic Solution:** Hydrocortisone, Fludrocortisone replacement therapy.

#### 157. Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)
* **Symptoms:** Irregular periods, excess facial hair, acne, weight gain, ovarian cysts.
* **Suggestions:** Low-GI diet, regular resistance training, weight management.
* **Basic Solution:** Combined oral contraceptives, Metformin, Anti-androgens (Spironolactone).

#### 158. Metabolic Syndrome
* **Symptoms:** Abdominal obesity, high blood pressure, high blood sugar, abnormal lipid levels.
* **Suggestions:** Lifestyle intervention, 150 minutes of weekly exercise, Mediterranean diet.
* **Basic Solution:** Statin therapy, antihypertensives, glucose-lowering agents.

#### 159. Gout (Hyperuricemia)
* **Symptoms:** Sudden, severe pain, redness, and swelling in joints (especially big toe).
* **Suggestions:** Avoid high-purine foods (red meat, seafood, alcohol), drink plenty of water.
* **Basic Solution:** Acute: NSAIDs, Colchicine, Steroids. Chronic: Allopurinol, Febuxostat.

#### 160. Osteoporosis
* **Symptoms:** Bone fractures from minor falls, stooped posture, loss of height over time.
* **Suggestions:** Calcium and Vitamin D-rich diet, weight-bearing exercises, avoid smoking.
* **Basic Solution:** Bisphosphonates (Alendronate), Denosumab, Teriparatide, SERMs.

*(Conditions 161 to 200 include: Gestational Diabetes, Acromegaly, Diabetes Insipidus, Hyperparathyroidism, Hypoparathyroidism, Hyperaldosteronism (Conn's Syndrome), Pheochromocytoma, Hashimoto's Encephalopathy, Subacute Thyroiditis, Thyroid Nodules, Primary Adrenal Hyperplasia, Hypopituitarism, Sheehan's Syndrome, Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia (MEN 1/2), Prolactinoma, Metabolic Acidosis, Metabolic Alkalosis, Hypercalcemia, Hypocalcemia, Hyperkalemia, Hypokalemia, Hyponatremia, Hypernatremia, Hypomagnesemia, Hypermagnesemia, Phenylketonuria (PKU), Wilson's Disease, Hemochromatosis, Gaucher Disease, Fabry Disease, Tay-Sachs Disease, Niemann-Pick Disease, Glycogen Storage Diseases, Homocystinuria, Maple Syrup Urine Disease, Porphyria, Familial Hypercholesterolemia, Amyloidosis, Obesity Class III, LADA (Latent Autoimmune Diabetes).*

### Category 5: Neurological & Brain Disorders (201–250)

#### 201. Migraine
* **Symptoms:** Throbbing headache (usually one-sided), nausea, sensitivity to light/sound, visual aura.
* **Suggestions:** Identify triggers (sleep changes, stress, bright lights), rest in a dark quiet room.
* **Basic Solution:** Acute: Triptans, NSAIDs. Preventive: CGRP inhibitors, Topiramate, Beta-blockers.

#### 202. Alzheimer's Disease
* **Symptoms:** Memory loss affecting daily life, confusion with time/place, difficulty solving problems.
* **Suggestions:** Cognitive stimulation, structured daily routines, safe home environment.
* **Basic Solution:** Cholinesterase inhibitors (Donepezil), NMDA antagonists (Memantine), Lecanemab.

#### 203. Parkinson's Disease
* **Symptoms:** Resting tremors, muscle rigidity, slow movements (bradykinesia), postural instability.
* **Suggestions:** Physical therapy, balance exercises, occupational adaptations.
* **Basic Solution:** Levodopa/Carbidopa, Dopamine agonists, MAO-B inhibitors, Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS).

#### 204. Epilepsy & Seizure Disorders
* **Symptoms:** Uncontrolled jerking movements, temporary confusion, staring spells, loss of consciousness.
* **Suggestions:** Adequate sleep, avoid strobe lights/stress, strict medication compliance.
* **Basic Solution:** Antiepileptic drugs (Levetiracetam, Lamotrigine, Valproate), vagus nerve stimulation.

#### 205. Multiple Sclerosis (MS)
* **Symptoms:** Numbness, weakness in limbs, vision loss/double vision, fatigue, gait issues.
* **Suggestions:** Cool environment (avoid heat flare-ups), physical therapy, fatigue management.
* **Basic Solution:** Disease-Modifying Therapies (Ocrelizumab, Interferons), IV steroids for relapses.

#### 206. Ischemic Stroke
* **Symptoms:** FAST (Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency).
* **Suggestions:** Immediate emergency hospital transfer.
* **Basic Solution:** IV tPA (tissue plasminogen activator) within window, Mechanical Thrombectomy, Aspirin.

#### 207. Tension Headache
* **Symptoms:** Dull, aching pain wrapped like a tight band around the forehead/head, neck stiffness.
* **Suggestions:** Neck stretch exercises, stress management, hydration, posture correction.
* **Basic Solution:** OTC analgesics (Acetaminophen, Ibuprofen), muscle relaxants.

#### 208. Peripheral Neuropathy
* **Symptoms:** Numbness, burning, stabbing pain, or tingling in hands and feet.
* **Suggestions:** Protect feet from injuries, tight blood sugar control (if diabetic).
* **Basic Solution:** Gabapentin, Pregabalin, Duloxetine, Topical Capsaicin.

#### 209. Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)
* **Symptoms:** Progressive muscle weakness, twitching (fasciculations), difficulty swallowing/speaking.
* **Suggestions:** Assistive technology, speech therapy, nutritional support via PEG tube.
* **Basic Solution:** Riluzole, Edaravone, supportive respiratory and multidisciplinary care.

#### 210. Bell's Palsy
* **Symptoms:** Sudden weakness or paralysis on one side of the face, drooping mouth, dry eye.
* **Suggestions:** Protect affected eye with lubricating drops and eye patch at night.
* **Basic Solution:** Oral Corticosteroids (Prednisone) started early, Antivirals in select cases.

*(Conditions 211 to 250 include: Trigeminal Neuralgia, Cluster Headache, Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus, Transient Ischemic Attack (TIA), Intracerebral Hemorrhage, Subarachnoid Hemorrhage, Guillain-Barré Syndrome, Myasthenia Gravis, Huntington's Disease, Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease, Essential Tremor, Restless Legs Syndrome, Narcolepsy, Restless Sleep Disorder, Carpal Tunnel Syndrome, Cubital Tunnel Syndrome, Sciatica, Meniere's Disease, Vestibular Neuritis, Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo (BPPV), Brain Aneurysm, Encephalitis, Bacterial Meningitis, Viral Meningitis, Spinal Stenosis, Cervical Radiculopathy, Lumbar Radiculopathy, Cerebral Palsy, Spina Bifida, Syringomyelia, Chiari Malformation, Neurofibromatosis Type 1/2, Tuberous Sclerosis, Restless Arm Syndrome, Post-Concussion Syndrome, Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI), Anoxic Brain Injury, Stiff-Person Syndrome, Restless Legs Syndrome, Neuromyelitis Optica).*

### Category 6: Dermatological & Skin Conditions (251–300)

#### 251. Acne Vulgaris
* **Symptoms:** Blackheads, whiteheads, pimples, deep painful cysts on face, chest, back.
* **Suggestions:** Non-comedogenic skincare, avoid touching face, gentle cleansing.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical Benzoyl Peroxide, Salicylic Acid, Retinoids, oral Isotretinoin for severe cases.

#### 252. Atopic Dermatitis (Eczema)
* **Symptoms:** Dry, red, intensely itchy skin patches, skin thickening/flaking.
* **Suggestions:** Frequent thick moisturizing (ceramides), avoid harsh soaps and hot showers.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical Corticosteroids, Topical Calcineurin Inhibitors, Dupilumab for severe cases.

#### 253. Psoriasis (Plaque)
* **Symptoms:** Raised red skin plaques covered with silvery scales, itching, joint pain (if psoriatic arthritis).
* **Suggestions:** Controlled sunlight exposure, moisturizing, stress reduction.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical Steroids, Vitamin D analogues, Phototherapy, Biologics (IL-17/IL-23 inhibitors).

#### 254. Rosacea
* **Symptoms:** Facial redness, visible blood vessels, bumps/pimples, flushing.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid triggers (spicy food, hot drinks, sunlight, alcohol), broad-spectrum sunscreen.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical Metronidazole, Azelaic Acid, Ivermectin, oral Doxycycline.

#### 255. Urticaria (Hives)
* **Symptoms:** Raised, itchy red or skin-colored welts that appear and disappear rapidly.
* **Suggestions:** Cool compresses, identify and avoid allergen triggers.
* **Basic Solution:** Second-generation H1 Antihistamines (Desloratadine, Fexofenadine), Omalizumab for chronic cases.

#### 256. Alopecia Areata
* **Symptoms:** Sudden spotty hair loss in round patches on scalp or body.
* **Suggestions:** Gentle hair care, managing psychological stress.
* **Basic Solution:** Intralesional Corticosteroid injections, Topical Minoxidil, JAK Inhibitors.

#### 257. Vitiligo
* **Symptoms:** Loss of skin pigment, producing chalk-white patches on skin and hair.
* **Suggestions:** High SPF sunscreen to prevent sunburn on depigmented areas.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical Corticosteroids/Tacrolimus, Narrowband UVB Phototherapy, Janus Kinase inhibitors.

#### 258. Tinea Pedis (Athlete's Foot)
* **Symptoms:** Itchy, peeling, cracking skin between toes and on foot soles.
* **Suggestions:** Keep feet clean and dry, change socks daily, wear breathable shoes.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical Antifungals (Terbinafine, Clotrimazole), oral antifungals if persistent.

#### 259. Contact Dermatitis
* **Symptoms:** Red rash, burning, itching, swelling after contact with a specific substance (e.g., Nickel, poison ivy).
* **Suggestions:** Wash skin immediately after exposure, avoid known allergen.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical hydrocortisone cream, oral antihistamines, barrier creams.

#### 260. Impetigo
* **Symptoms:** Red sores around nose/mouth that break open and form honey-colored crusts.
* **Suggestions:** Maintain strict hygiene, avoid sharing towels/clothes.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical Mupirocin ointment, oral antibiotics (Cephalexin) for widespread sores.

### Category 7: Musculoskeletal & Rheumatological Disorders (301–350)

#### 301. Osteoarthritis
* **Symptoms:** Joint pain during movement, stiffness in morning (<30 mins), joint crepitus, bone spurs.
* **Suggestions:** Weight management, low-impact exercise (swimming, cycling), joint protection.
* **Basic Solution:** Topical/Oral NSAIDs, Acetaminophen, Intra-articular Hyaluronic Acid or Steroid injections.

#### 302. Rheumatoid Arthritis
* **Symptoms:** Symmetric joint swelling, warmth, pain, morning stiffness lasting >1 hour, fatigue.
* **Suggestions:** Joint preservation techniques, gentle range-of-motion exercises.
* **Basic Solution:** DMARDs (Methotrexate, Sulfasalazine), Biologics (TNF inhibitors), short-term steroids.

#### 303. Ankylosing Spondylitis
* **Symptoms:** Chronic inflammatory back pain, stiffness easing with exercise, reduced spine flexibility.
* **Suggestions:** Daily posture and spinal stretching exercises, firm mattress usage.
* **Basic Solution:** NSAIDs (first-line), Biologics (Secukinumab, Adalimumab), physical therapy.

#### 304. Fibromyalgia
* **Symptoms:** Widespread musculoskeletal pain, chronic fatigue, sleep disturbance, cognitive fibro-fog.
* **Suggestions:** Moderate regular exercise, sleep hygiene, stress management techniques.
* **Basic Solution:** Pregabalin, Duloxetine, Milnacipran, multidisciplinary pain management.

#### 305. Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE)
* **Symptoms:** Butterfly rash on face, joint pain, fatigue, fever, kidney involvement.
* **Suggestions:** Sun protection (UV light triggers flares), adequate rest, infection prevention.
* **Basic Solution:** Hydroxychloroquine, Immunosuppressants (Mycophenolate), Corticosteroids, Belimumab.

#### 306. Tendonitis (e.g., Achilles, Rotator Cuff)
* **Symptoms:** Dull ache at joint tendon, tenderness, mild swelling with movement.
* **Suggestions:** R.I.C.E protocol (Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation), eccentric loading exercises.
* **Basic Solution:** NSAIDs, physical therapy, corticosteroid injections around tendon sheath.

#### 307. Plantar Fasciitis
* **Symptoms:** Stabbing heel pain with first steps in the morning or after sitting.
* **Suggestions:** Calf and plantar fascia stretching, supportive footwear with arch support.
* **Basic Solution:** Orthotic shoe inserts, night splints, NSAIDs, physical therapy, shockwave therapy.

#### 308. Cervical Spondylosis
* **Symptoms:** Neck pain, stiffness, radiating arm pain, numbness in fingers.
* **Suggestions:** Ergonomic desk setup, neck strengthening exercises, posture maintenance.
* **Basic Solution:** Analgesics, muscle relaxants, physical therapy, cervical collar for short term.

#### 309. Sarcopenia
* **Symptoms:** Muscle weakness, loss of stamina, slow walking speed, frailty.
* **Suggestions:** Progressive resistance training, high-protein nutrition plan.
* **Basic Solution:** Vitamin D supplementation, targeted protein/essential amino acid therapy.

#### 310. Polymyalgia Rheumatica
* **Symptoms:** Severe stiffness and pain in shoulders, neck, and hips, especially mornings.
* **Suggestions:** Gentle active movement, avoiding sudden heavy physical exertion.
* **Basic Solution:** Low-to-moderate dose oral Corticosteroids (rapid symptom relief).

*(Conditions 311 to 350 include: Psoriatic Arthritis, Reactive Arthritis, Scleroderma (Systemic Sclerosis), Sjogren's Syndrome, Dermatomyositis, Polymyositis, Bursitis, Carpal Tunnel Syndrome, Dupuytren's Contracture, Trigger Finger, Adhesive Capsulitis (Frozen Shoulder), Patellofemoral Pain Syndrome, Shin Splints, Compartment Syndrome, Spinal Stenosis, Spondylolisthesis, Herniated Disc, Kyphosis, Scoliosis, Lordosis, Osteomyelitis, Septic Arthritis, Paget's Disease of Bone, Osteomalacia, Rickets, Behcet's Disease, Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis, Giant Cell Arteritis, Relapsing Polychondritis, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, Marfan Syndrome, Osteogenesis Imperfecta, Torticollis, Baker's Cyst, Morton's Neuroma, Tennis Elbow (Lateral Epicondylitis), Golfer's Elbow (Medial Epicondylitis), De Quervain's Tenosynovitis, Ganglion Cyst, Costochondritis).*

### Category 8: Renal & Urological Disorders (351–400)

#### 351. Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)
* **Symptoms:** Decreased urine output, fluid retention/swelling, fatigue, confusion, nausea.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid nephrotoxic drugs, ensure proper hydration.
* **Basic Solution:** Treating underlying cause, fluid balancing, temporary hemodialysis if severe.

#### 352. Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)
* **Symptoms:** Fatigue, swollen feet/ankles, nausea, metallic taste, changes in urination frequency.
* **Suggestions:** Low-sodium, low-protein, controlled potassium/phosphorus diet.
* **Basic Solution:** ACE inhibitors/ARBs, SGLT2 inhibitors, Erythropoietin for anemia, Dialysis/Transplant (Stage 5).

#### 353. Nephrolithiasis (Kidney Stones)
* **Symptoms:** Severe sharp flank pain radiating to groin, blood in urine, nausea, painful urination.
* **Suggestions:** High water intake (2.5-3L daily), limit sodium and animal protein.
* **Basic Solution:** Alpha-blockers (Tamsulosin) to pass stone, Analgesics, Lithotripsy (ESWL), Ureteroscopy.

#### 354. Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
* **Symptoms:** Burning sensation during urination, frequent urge to urinate, cloudy/smelly urine.
* **Suggestions:** Hydrate heavily, don't hold urine, wipe front-to-back, cranberry extracts.
* **Basic Solution:** Antibiotics (Nitrofurantoin, Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole), Phenazopyridine for pain.

#### 355. Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH)
* **Symptoms:** Weak urine stream, difficulty starting urination, frequent nighttime urination (nocturia).
* **Suggestions:** Limit fluids before bedtime, cut caffeine and alcohol.
* **Basic Solution:** Alpha-1 Blockers (Tamsulosin), 5-alpha Reductase Inhibitors (Finasteride), TURP surgery.

#### 356. Interstitial Cystitis (Painful Bladder Syndrome)
* **Symptoms:** Chronic pelvic pain, bladder pressure, urgent need to urinate frequently small amounts.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid bladder irritants (citrus, tomatoes, coffee, artificial sweeteners).
* **Basic Solution:** Pentosan Polysulfate Sodium, bladder instillations, antihistamines, nerve stimulation.

#### 357. Urinary Incontinence (Stress / Urge)
* **Symptoms:** Involuntary urine leakage during coughing/exercise or sudden intense urge.
* **Suggestions:** Pelvic floor (Kegel) exercises, bladder training schedules, weight loss.
* **Basic Solution:** Anticholinergics (Oxybutynin), Mirabegron, pessaries, surgical slings.

#### 358. Glomerulonephritis
* **Symptoms:** Pink/cola-colored urine (hematuria), foamy urine (proteinuria), high blood pressure, facial swelling.
* **Suggestions:** Low-salt diet, fluid management, blood pressure monitoring.
* **Basic Solution:** Immunosuppressants, Corticosteroids, blood pressure medications.

#### 359. Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD)
* **Symptoms:** High blood pressure, back/side pain, enlarged abdomen, kidney stones, blood in urine.
* **Suggestions:** Strict blood pressure control, high fluid intake, low-salt diet.
* **Basic Solution:** Tolvaptan (to slow cyst growth), antihypertensives, pain management.

#### 360. Epididymitis
* **Symptoms:** Swollen, red, or warm scrotum, testicle pain, painful urination/ejaculation.
* **Suggestions:** Scrotal support, ice packs, rest.
* **Basic Solution:** Antibiotics (Doxycycline, Ceftriaxone), pain relievers.

*(Conditions 361 to 400 include: Pyelonephritis, Bladder Cancer, Renal Cell Carcinoma, Prostate Cancer, Testicular Torsion, Hydrocele, Varicocele, Spermatocele, Phimosis, Paraphimosis, Erectile Dysfunction, Peyronie's Disease, Priapism, Overactive Bladder (OAB), Neurogenic Bladder, Urethral Stricture, Renal Tubular Acidosis, Fanconi Syndrome, Alport Syndrome, IgA Nephropathy (Berger's Disease), Membranous Nephropathy, Minimal Change Disease, Focal Segmental Glomerulosclerosis (FSGS), Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS), Renal Artery Thrombosis, Renal Vein Thrombosis, Uremic Pericarditis, Hydronephrosis, Vesicoureteral Reflux, Cystitis, Prostatitis (Acute/Chronic), Orchitis, Balanitis, Renal Cyst (Simple), Retroperitoneal Fibrosis, Bladder Exstrophy, Enuresis (Bedwetting), Urethritis, Ureteral Calculus, Radiation Cystitis).*

### Category 9: Ophthalmic & Ear, Nose, Throat (ENT) Disorders (401–450)

#### 401. Cataract
* **Symptoms:** Clouded, blurred vision, difficulty with night vision, light sensitivity, faded colors.
* **Suggestions:** Wear UV-blocking sunglasses, use bright reading lights.
* **Basic Solution:** Surgical phacoemulsification with Intraocular Lens (IOL) implantation.

#### 402. Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma
* **Symptoms:** Gradual loss of peripheral vision, patchy blind spots, painless progression.
* **Suggestions:** Regular comprehensive eye exams (tonometry).
* **Basic Solution:** Prostaglandin analog eye drops (Latanoprost), Beta-blocker drops, Laser trabeculoplasty.

#### 403. Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD)
* **Symptoms:** Distorted vision (straight lines appear wavy), loss of central vision clarity.
* **Suggestions:** AREDS2 formula supplements (Lutein, Zeaxanthin, Zinc, Vit C/E), stop smoking.
* **Basic Solution:** Anti-VEGF intravitreal injections (Ranibizumab, Aflibercept) for wet AMD.

#### 404. Diabetic Retinopathy
* **Symptoms:** Spots or dark strings floating in vision (floaters), fluctuating vision, impaired color vision.
* **Suggestions:** Strict blood sugar, blood pressure, and cholesterol control.
* **Basic Solution:** Anti-VEGF injections, retinal laser photocoagulation, vitrectomy.

#### 405. Otitis Media (Middle Ear Infection)
* **Symptoms:** Ear pain, tugging at ear (in kids), fluid drainage, muffled hearing, fever.
* **Suggestions:** Warm compress on ear, maintain dry ear.
* **Basic Solution:** Analgesic ear drops, oral antibiotics (Amoxicillin) if severe or persistent.

#### 406. Meniere's Disease
* **Symptoms:** Episodes of spinning vertigo, ringing in ear (tinnitus), hearing loss, feeling of ear fullness.
* **Suggestions:** Low-salt diet, avoid caffeine, alcohol, and tobacco.
* **Basic Solution:** Diuretics (Hydrochlorothiazide), Betahistine, anti-vertigo drugs (Meclizine).

#### 407. Tinnitus
* **Symptoms:** Perception of ringing, buzzing, hissing, or roaring sound in ears without external source.
* **Suggestions:** White noise machines, avoiding exposure to loud noises, stress reduction.
* **Basic Solution:** Sound masking devices, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), addressing root cause.

#### 408. Dry Eye Syndrome
* **Symptoms:** Stinging, burning sensation, scratchy feeling, red eyes, watery eyes (reflex).
* **Suggestions:** Follow 20-20-20 screen rule, use humidifiers, avoid direct fan draft.
* **Basic Solution:** Artificial tears, Cyclosporine eye drops, Punctal plugs.

#### 409. Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)
* **Symptoms:** Redness in white of eye, itching, tearing, discharge forming crust overnight.
* **Suggestions:** Wash hands frequently, refrain from rubbing eyes, throw away old eye makeup.
* **Basic Solution:** Viral: Cold compress; Bacterial: Antibiotic drops; Allergic: Antihistamine drops.

#### 410. Vertigo (BPPV)
* **Symptoms:** Brief intense sensation that room is spinning triggered by head movements.
* **Suggestions:** Move head slowly, avoid sudden bending.
* **Basic Solution:** Epley maneuver (canalith repositioning procedure), vestibular rehabilitation.

*(Conditions 411 to 450 include: Otitis Externa (Swimmer's Ear), Laryngitis, Pharyngitis, Nasal Polyps, Deviated Nasal Septum, Strabismus, Amblyopia, Blepharitis, Chalazion, Stye (Hordeolum), Uveitis, Keratitis, Pterygium, Retinal Detachment, Retinitis Pigmentosa, Optic Neuritis, Acoustic Neuroma, Presbycusis, Sudden Sensorineural Hearing Loss, Tympanic Membrane Perforation, Mastoiditis, Sialadenitis, Salivary Gland Stones, Tonsillar Hypertrophy, Adenoiditis, Vocal Cord Polyps, Epistaxis (Nosebleed), Anosmia, Ageusia, Otosclerosis, Cholesteatoma, Endophthalmitis, Scleritis, Episcleritis, Corneal Ulcer, Ectropion, Entropion, Ptosis, Central Serous Chorioretinopathy, Graves' Ophthalmopathy).*

### Category 10: Hematological & Immunological Disorders (451–500)

#### 451. Iron Deficiency Anemia
* **Symptoms:** Extreme fatigue, pale skin, weakness, cold hands/feet, brittle nails, pica (craving ice/dirt).
* **Suggestions:** Eat iron-rich foods (red meat, spinach, lentils) paired with Vitamin C.
* **Basic Solution:** Oral Ferrous Sulfate / Fumarate supplements, IV iron infusions in severe cases.

#### 452. Vitamin B12 Deficiency Anemia (Pernicious Anemia)
* **Symptoms:** Fatigue, numbness/tingling in hands/feet, smooth red tongue, memory loss.
* **Suggestions:** Include dairy, eggs, meat or fortified foods (for vegans).
* **Basic Solution:** High-dose oral Cyanocobalamin or intramuscular Vitamin B12 injections.

#### 453. Sickle Cell Disease
* **Symptoms:** Severe painful episodes (pain crises), anemia, frequent infections, swelling of hands/feet.
* **Suggestions:** Stay highly hydrated, avoid high altitudes and extreme temperature changes.
* **Basic Solution:** Hydroxyurea, pain relievers, blood transfusions, L-glutamine, Gene therapy.

#### 454. Idiopathic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (ITP)
* **Symptoms:** Easy bruising (purpura), pinpoint red spots on skin (petechiae), bleeding gums, heavy periods.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid contact sports, avoid Aspirin/NSAIDs that increase bleeding risk.
* **Basic Solution:** Corticosteroids, IVIG (Intravenous Immunoglobulin), Thrombopoietin receptor agonists.

#### 455. Hemophilia A / B
* **Symptoms:** Uncontrolled bleeding after injuries, large deep bruises, joint pain/swelling from internal bleeding.
* **Suggestions:** Protect joints, wear helmet/protective gear, avoid blood-thinning OTC meds.
* **Basic Solution:** Clotting factor replacement therapy (Factor VIII for A, Factor IX for B), Desmopressin.

#### 456. Aplastic Anemia
* **Symptoms:** Fatigue, frequent infections, easy bleeding/bruising, pale skin.
* **Suggestions:** Avoid crowded spaces to reduce infection risk, rest frequently.
* **Basic Solution:** Blood transfusions, Immunosuppressive therapy (ATG + Cyclosporine), Bone Marrow Transplant.

#### 457. Hereditary Angioedema (HAE)
* **Symptoms:** Recurrent episodes of severe swelling in hands, feet, face, airway, or intestinal tract.
* **Suggestions:** Identify stress/trauma triggers, carry emergency medical ID card.
* **Basic Solution:** C1 inhibitor concentrate, Ecallantide, Icatibant (bradykinin receptor antagonist).

#### 458. Deep Neutropenia
* **Symptoms:** Frequent fevers, severe recurrent infections, mouth ulcers.
* **Suggestions:** Strict neutropenic diet (cooked foods only), meticulous hand hygiene.
* **Basic Solution:** Granulocyte-colony stimulating factor (G-CSF) injections (e.g., Filgrastim).

#### 459. Polycythemia Vera
* **Symptoms:** Itching (especially after warm shower), headaches, dizziness, reddened skin, enlarged spleen.
* **Suggestions:** Adequate hydration, light walking to prevent blood clots.
* **Basic Solution:** Phlebotomy (blood removal), low-dose Aspirin, Hydroxyurea, Ruxolitinib.

#### 460. Common Variable Immunodeficiency (CVID)
* **Symptoms:** Recurrent lung, sinus, and ear infections, chronic diarrhea, enlarged lymph nodes.
* **Suggestions:** Practice strict hygiene, avoid raw or unpasteurized foods.
* **Basic Solution:** Regular Immunoglobulin replacement therapy (IVIG or SCIG), prophylactic antibiotics.

*(Conditions 461 to 500 include: Thalassemia Major/Minor, Von Willebrand Disease, Multiple Myeloma, Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL), Chronic Myeloid Leukemia (CML), Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia (ALL), Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML), Hodgkin Lymphoma, Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma, Myelofibrosis, Essential Thrombocythemia, Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC), Antiphospholipid Syndrome, Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (TTP), Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria (PNH), Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia, Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase (G6PD) Deficiency, Sideroblastic Anemia, Megaloblastic Anemia, Anemia of Chronic Disease, Mastocytosis, Severe Combined Immunodeficiency (SCID), Wiskott-Aldrich Syndrome, DiGeorge Syndrome, X-linked Agammaglobulinemia, Hyper-IgE Syndrome, Chédiak-Higashi Syndrome, Hemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis (HLH), Lymphostasis, Castleman Disease, Cryoglobulinemia, Monoclonal Gammopathy of Undetermined Significance (MGUS), Cold Agglutinin Disease, Factor V Leiden Mutation, Protein C Deficiency, Protein S Deficiency, Antithrombin III Deficiency, Eosinophilia, Leukocytosis, Leukopenia).*
